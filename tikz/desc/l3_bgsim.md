Simulated output of a Brokaw bandgap reference against temperature.

One curve on axes of temperature in degrees Celsius across and $V_{BG}$
up. The curve rises from the cold end, peaks a little above room
temperature, and falls away towards the hot end. A dashed horizontal
line is drawn across it and labelled "typical trim point".

The vertical scale is heavily magnified: the whole range of the curve is
a few millivolts on an output near 1.2 V. The bow is the second order
error left after the linear term has been cancelled, not a large
variation.
