Two ideal source symbols side by side, each drawn as a vertical branch
between two open terminals.

Left, the voltage source: a circle with a plus at the top terminal and a
minus at the bottom, labelled $V_S$, captioned "Voltage source".

Right, the current source: the same vertical branch with a current
source symbol, labelled $I_S$, captioned "Current source". Its terminals
carry no polarity marks.
